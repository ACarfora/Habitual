# Design System Document

## Core Principles

Our design system is built upon clarity, consistency, and a refined aesthetic. We aim for an intuitive user experience across all touchpoints.

## Theme Configuration

The current theme is configured with a **light** color mode, emphasizing a bright and accessible interface.

### Colors

Our color palette is carefully selected to evoke a sense of earthy sophistication and professionalism.

*   **Primary Color**: `#4B5340` - A deep, forest green that serves as our primary brand accent.
*   **Secondary Color**: `#8C8376` - A warm, muted beige, providing a soft contrast and support.
*   **Tertiary Color**: `#2C2C2C` - A very dark grey, used for subtle accents and depth.
*   **Neutral Color**: `#E8E6E1` - A light, almost off-white, forming the foundation of our backgrounds and surfaces.

### Typography

We employ a balanced selection of typefaces to ensure readability and brand expression.

*   **Headlines**: `Manrope` - Modern and highly legible, perfect for impactful headings.
*   **Body Text**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` - Clean and versatile, ideal for extended reading.
*   **Labels**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` - Consistent with body text for clarity in UI elements.

### Shape & Form

The visual language incorporates a **moderate roundedness (2)** for UI elements, offering a contemporary and friendly feel without being overly soft.

### Spacing

We maintain a **normal spacing (2)** within and between UI elements, striking a balance between density and spaciousness to enhance readability and visual comfort.
